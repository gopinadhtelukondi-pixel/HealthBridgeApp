SMART DOCTOR RANKING SYSTEM - STEP BY STEP IMPLEMENTATION GUIDE
===============================================================

Project: HealthBridge - Transparent Healthcare Decision Platform
Goal: Rank doctors ethically and intelligently using multiple healthcare decision factors, not only star ratings.


1. WHAT WE ARE BUILDING
-----------------------

The Smart Doctor Ranking System will convert patient review data into useful doctor discovery signals.

Instead of ranking doctors only by rating, we will calculate a balanced trust score using:

- Patient satisfaction
- Treatment recovery/success reports
- Communication quality
- Affordability
- Waiting time
- Recommendation percentage
- Experience years
- Verified patient review count
- Ethical transparency signals

The result will help patients find doctors by real needs:

- Best patient care
- Best affordable doctor
- Low cost high success
- Top rated specialist
- Short waiting time
- Best communication
- Most recommended


2. WHY THIS SHOULD NOT BE SIMPLE STAR RANKING
---------------------------------------------

Only using average rating is risky because:

- A doctor with 2 reviews can appear better than a doctor with 300 reviews.
- Expensive doctors may get high ratings but may not be affordable.
- A polite doctor may not always have strong recovery outcomes.
- A highly skilled doctor may have long waiting times.
- Some reviews may be fake, emotional, incomplete, or biased.

So we need weighted ranking.

The ranking must be:

- Transparent
- Explainable
- Ethical
- Moderation-aware
- Based only on approved reviews
- Helpful, not defamatory


3. DATA SOURCES
---------------

Primary data comes from:

Review collection:

Doctor collection:

Only reviews with moderationStatus = "approved" should affect public ranking.


4. CORE SCORE TYPES
-------------------

We will calculate these scores for each doctor:

1. patientSatisfactionScore

Based on:

- average overallRating
- recommendation percentage
- positive sentiment percentage

2. recoveryScore

Based on:

- percentage of reviews where treatmentWorked = true
- recovery-related review feedback

3. communicationScore

Based on:

- average doctorCommunication rating

4. affordabilityScore

Based on:

- average approximateCost from reviews
- doctor consultation fee
- comparison with other doctors in same specialty/city

5. waitingTimeScore

Based on:

- lower average waiting time = higher score

6. experienceScore

Based on:

- years of experience
- capped so very high experience does not dominate everything

7. trustVolumeScore

Based on:

- number of approved verified reviews
- gives confidence to doctors with more reliable data

8. transparencyScore

Based on:

- verified doctor profile
- available fee
- available education
- available hospital
- review count


5. FINAL TRUST SCORE FORMULA
----------------------------

Example weighted formula:

trustScore =

- patientSatisfactionScore * 0.25
- recoveryScore * 0.20
- communicationScore * 0.15
- affordabilityScore * 0.15
- waitingTimeScore * 0.10
- experienceScore * 0.07
- trustVolumeScore * 0.05
- transparencyScore * 0.03

Total = 100%

The final score should be normalized from 0 to 100.

Important:

Weights can be adjusted later after testing.


6. SCORE NORMALIZATION
----------------------

All score components should become 0 to 100.

Examples:

Rating score:

averageRating / 5 * 100

Recommendation score:

recommendedReviews / totalReviews * 100

Recovery score:

treatmentWorkedReviews / totalReviews * 100

Communication score:

averageCommunication / 5 * 100

Waiting time score:

- 0 to 15 minutes = 100
- 16 to 30 minutes = 85
- 31 to 45 minutes = 70
- 46 to 60 minutes = 55
- above 60 minutes = 40

Experience score:

- 0 years = 20
- 5 years = 50
- 10 years = 75
- 15+ years = 100

Trust volume score:

- 0 reviews = 0
- 1 to 4 reviews = 25
- 5 to 19 reviews = 60
- 20 to 49 reviews = 80
- 50+ reviews = 100


7. AFFORDABILITY SCORE
----------------------

Affordability must compare similar doctors.

Example:

Compare doctor cost against doctors with same:

- specialty
- city

Use:

- doctor fee
- average patient-reported treatment cost

Simple first version:

If doctor consultation fee is:

- lowest 25% in specialty/city: score 100
- below average: score 80
- near average: score 65
- above average: score 45
- very expensive: score 25

Later version:

Use treatment-specific cost comparisons.


8. RANKING LABELS
-----------------

After scores are calculated, generate labels.

Examples:

Best Patient Care:

- patientSatisfactionScore >= 85
- communicationScore >= 80
- recommendationPercentage >= 80

Best Affordable Doctor:

- affordabilityScore >= 85
- patientSatisfactionScore >= 70

Low Cost High Success:

- affordabilityScore >= 80
- recoveryScore >= 80

Top Rated Specialist:

- trustScore >= 85
- approvedReviewCount >= 5

Short Waiting Time:

- waitingTimeScore >= 85

Best Communication:

- communicationScore >= 90

Most Recommended:

- recommendationPercentage >= 90
- approvedReviewCount >= 5

Needs More Data:

- approvedReviewCount < 3

Important:

If a doctor has very few reviews, do not overclaim.
Use label: "Limited review data".


9. BACKEND IMPLEMENTATION
-------------------------

Create:

server/services/rankingService.js

Functions:

calculateDoctorRanking(doctor)
calculateDoctorsRanking(doctors)
getDoctorRankingSummary(doctorId)

This service should:

- Fetch approved reviews for each doctor.
- Calculate average review metrics.
- Normalize scores.
- Calculate final trustScore.
- Generate ranking labels.
- Return ranking summary.

Returns ranking summary for one doctor.

14. IMPORTANT ETHICAL WORDING
-----------------------------

Avoid:

- "This is the best doctor."
- "This doctor is bad."
- "Guaranteed success."
- "Avoid this doctor."

Use:

- "Highly rated by patients."
- "Based on patient-reported experiences."
- "Limited review data."
- "Reported waiting time is higher than average."
- "Costs appear above average for similar doctors."

Always show:

"Scores are based on approved patient-reported reviews and profile data. They are decision-support signals, not medical advice."


15. LOW REVIEW COUNT HANDLING
-----------------------------

If approvedReviewCount = 0:

- Use doctor profile data only.
- Show: "No patient review data yet."
- Do not show strong ranking labels.

If approvedReviewCount < 3:

- Show: "Limited review data."
- Reduce trustVolumeScore.
- Do not show "Top Rated" label.

If approvedReviewCount >= 5:

- Stronger labels can appear.
